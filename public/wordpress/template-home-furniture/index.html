03 · The Forma manifesto

We believe furniture should outlive you. Every Oka piece is dimensioned by hand, every mortise cut with the same tools our masters used in the 1950s — no CNC shortcuts, no veneer, no hardware.

Walnut comes from the Hida valley. Ash from Aomori. Oak from Kirishima. Each plank air-dries a minimum of three years before we even look at it. If the grain isn't right, we don't use it.

When the wood is ready, we apply eight coats of hand-rubbed tung oil. Nothing between you and the tree. No lacquer. No film. It only gets better with age.

— H. Oka
Founded MCMLXXIV Hiroshi Oka, 3rd-generation master joiner

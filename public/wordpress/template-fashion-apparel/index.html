02 · Crafted, Not Manufactured

Fourteen mills.
One standard.

Every MAISON NOIR garment begins the same way — at a family-run mill we've partnered with for years. Biella for wools, Okayama for denims, Normandy for linens. We don't chase seasons or reorders; we buy exactly what our artisans can produce at their best pace.

The result is a smaller, more honest collection. Pieces that arrive when they're ready, priced closer to what it costs to make them, built to wear beautifully as they age.

G.L.
Genevieve Laurent Founder & Creative Director

A Day at Mill Number 7.

We spend a day with the Rinaldi family, whose mill in Biella has been carding and spinning virgin wool since the summer of 1948. Four generations, one loom room, no shortcuts.
